Hardwood Floor Water Damage: Dry and Refinish, or Replace?

Most hardwood can be dried and refinished when the water was clean, the response was quick and the boards flatten back out. Replacement makes sense when boards stay buckled, rot, or were soaked with sewage.

Water restoration is the full process of returning a property to its condition before the loss. It starts with stopping damage, moves through drying and cleanup, and ends with repairs and rebuild.

Renters Insurance and Water Damage: What Tenants Should Know

A renters policy usually covers your belongings and extra living costs after sudden water damage, while the landlord's policy covers the building. Flood and slow leaks are the usual gaps. Check your own policy.

How to Kill Mold on Wood (and When Cleaning Is Not Enough)

Light surface mold on sealed or finished wood can often be cleaned by a careful homeowner. Raw, soft or structural wood is different. Moisture has to be fixed first, or the mold has what it needs to return.

Mold

How Do You Get Rid of Mold in Your Home?

You get rid of household mold by fixing the moisture source first, then containing the area, removing or cleaning affected materials, drying everything, and checking the result before rebuilding.

Mold

What Kills Black Mold Instantly? Myths and Facts

Nothing removes mold instantly. Sprays can kill surface growth on hard materials, but mold returns where moisture stays. The lasting fix is drying the source and removing what cannot be cleaned.
