What to say when you call

Have these ready

  • ✓Your address and the nearest cross street
  • ✓Where the water started and what it touched
  • ✓Whether it is clean water, dark water or sewage
  • ✓Whether water is near outlets, the panel or appliances
  • ✓Whether you own, rent or manage the property

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Before you call

If you can reach the main water valve safely, shut it off. If water is near electrical equipment, stay out and call the utility or 911 first. Photograph the damage before moving anything. You can read more steps on the emergency water removal page.
