About South Gate Water Damage Pros in South Gate, CA

A South Gate-based emergency line for water damage, flood and mold restoration, serving six Los Angeles County cities.

☎ Call (213) 776-0579

Who we are

South Gate Water Damage Pros is a restoration service based at 4460 Firestone Boulevard in South Gate, California. We take emergency calls about water damage, flooding and mold, and we send licensed and insured restoration professionals to homes and businesses in South Gate, Downey, Lynwood, Huntington Park, Paramount and Compton.

The phone line is answered 24 hours a day, every day of the year, because water losses do not wait for business hours. Same-day response is available when crews can be spared, and we explain scope and price before any work starts.

How a call works

  1. You call (213) 776-0579 and describe the source, the rooms affected and any safety risk.
  2. The dispatcher gives you immediate steps, such as where to shut off water and what to keep out of.
  3. A crew is sent as soon as one is available, and the crew lead explains the scope and price before starting.
  4. The crew extracts water, dries the structure with measured readings and documents the loss.
  5. Repairs follow once the structure is dry, and mold work is planned separately if it is needed.

What we do not do

Being honest about limits is part of the job. We do not waterproof foundations, repair roofs or fix leaking pipes. Those belong to waterproofing, roofing and plumbing trades, and we will tell you when the source of your leak needs one of them before restoration can last.

We do not diagnose illness, and the mold pages on this site make no health claims. We do not decide insurance coverage, and we cannot tell you what your policy will pay. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and see the water mitigation page for the records worth keeping.

Standards we point to

Two published industry standards guide this work. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers professional water damage restoration, including the water categories and drying goals. ANSI/IICRC S520 covers professional mold remediation. We name them so you can ask any company you are considering whether its crews work to them.

Our guidance on mold remediation and flood damage restoration leans on public sources too, including EPA mold guidance, the California Department of Insurance and the City of South Gate.

How this website is written

Every page is written for this part of Los Angeles County. Local facts, such as the Los Angeles River running through the eastern part of South Gate, the City's hard groundwater and the Building and Safety Division's permit rules, come from city, state and federal sources. When a fact cannot be confirmed, we leave it out or tell you to check your own records.

We do not publish customer reviews, star ratings or job counts, because we would rather show you how the work is done than ask you to trust a number. Pages are signed by the team, not by an individual.

What to expect from the crew on site

A crew lead walks the property with you first. They look at the source, the water category and the rooms involved, then check moisture with a meter and a thermal imaging camera. You should hear a plain-language scope before anything is moved, along with the main cost factors and the likely number of drying days.

During the job, crews protect floors and furniture you are keeping, tell you where equipment will run and why, and keep dated photos and readings. At the end you should have a record of what was removed, what was dried and what still needs rebuilding. If something changes, such as hidden damage behind a wall, the crew lead explains it before adding work.

Questions to ask any restoration company

Worth asking before you agree to work

  • ✓What licenses does the company hold, and can I look them up on the California Contractors State License Board website?
  • ✓Do your crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500 for water and S520 for mold?
  • ✓Will you explain scope and price before work starts, and put them in writing?
  • ✓What moisture readings will you take, and how will I see them?
  • ✓What does your documentation look like for my insurer?
  • ✓Who fixes the source of the leak, and who handles the rebuild?

A company that answers these plainly is easier to trust than one that rushes you to sign. Use the same list with us, and ask for the answers on the phone before a crew is sent.
