Postwar homes in Downey and the pipes inside them

Downey was incorporated in 1956, and much of its housing grew out of the farmland and suburban expansion that followed World War II. Postwar tract homes commonly have copper supply lines and cast-iron or clay drain lines, which age differently from the plastic piping used in newer construction. When a line fails behind a wall, the age of the pipe is worth mentioning when you call.

Water categories on a Downey job follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 industry standard, so clean supply-line water and contaminated water are handled differently from the first step.

Downey's 2020 census count was 114,355 residents in roughly 36,000 housing units, split about evenly between owners and renters. That mix matters when water arrives, because half the city's households need to involve a landlord or property manager as well as an insurer. Photograph the damage first and send the landlord written notice the same day.

When water is spreading in Downey

A burst line, an overflowing washer or a failed water heater turns into a large loss fast, so call as soon as you see it. Our 24/7 line is answered at night and on weekends, and same-day response is available when crews can be spared. Describe where the water started and where it is going, and tell the dispatcher whether it is near outlets or has an odor.

Between two rivers: flood questions for Downey owners

Downey sits between the Rio Hondo and the San Gabriel River. That does not make every address a flood risk, but it makes the question worth asking. Look up your street on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask your insurer whether your policy covers flood, since homeowners policies usually exclude it and a flood policy generally waits 30 days to take effect.

If water has already entered a home from outside, treat it as contaminated and read our flood damage restoration page before touching anything. Standing water inside is covered on the emergency water removal page.

If water is still spreading: before we arrive

1

Stop the source

Shut off the main water valve, or the valve at the fixture, if you can reach it without standing in water.

2

Mind the power

Cut power to wet areas only if the panel is dry. For any electrical or gas risk, call the utility or 911 first.

3

Lift what you can

Raise furniture, electronics and papers off the floor and onto dry surfaces.

4

Keep the area clear

Keep children and pets out of dark or foul water and take dated photos before anything moves.

What Downey property owners call about most

Because of the river setting and the age of the housing stock, calls tend to start with water mitigation, the drying that begins in the first 24 to 72 hours, and move into water damage repair once the structure is dry. Drying takes careful readings, not guesses. Wet rooms that stay damp past two days may need the mold steps on the mold remediation page.
