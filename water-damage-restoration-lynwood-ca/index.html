A farm town that became a suburb: what that means indoors

Lynwood was incorporated in 1921 and grew from a farming town into a suburb between about 1940 and 1970. Homes from the mid-century decades commonly have copper supply lines, cast-iron drains and plaster or early drywall walls, all of which fail in their own ways. If a home shows brown stains on a ceiling or a damp baseboard, tell the crew when it was built.

About 46.6 percent of Lynwood's occupied homes are owner-occupied, which means a little over half are rented. Renters should send written notice to the landlord and keep dated photos, while owners should call their insurer early. Either way, document the damage before the crew starts.

Why speed matters more than the cleanup plan

People often spend the first hour of a leak deciding who to call and how much it might cost. That hour is when drywall soaks through and baseboards swell. Call first, then compare. EPA guidance says to dry or discard wet materials within 24 to 48 hours to limit mold growth, so a crew that starts drying the same day gives you far more options than one that starts on day three.

A short call also lets the dispatcher tell you what to do in the meantime. Whether that is closing a valve, moving a rug or staying out of a room, the advice is quick and often saves the carpet. Keep your phone near you and your photos dated.
