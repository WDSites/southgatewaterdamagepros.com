Water heater leak emergency: what to do right now

When a tank water heater leaks, the water spreads from the base across the garage slab, closet floor or hallway and soaks whatever sits low. Close the cold water inlet valve on the pipe feeding the tank, set the heater to off, and call our 24/7 line at (213) 776-0579. A licensed plumber repairs or replaces the heater. We extract the water, dry the building and clean up what it touched.

Safety first: cold inlet, gas, power and hot water

Start with the cold water inlet, usually a handle or wheel on the pipe at the top of the tank. Turning it clockwise stops new water from refilling the tank. If the handle will not move, shut the main water valve for the house instead.

  • Gas heater: turn the gas control to off or pilot. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside.
  • Electric heater: switch off its breaker, but only if the panel is dry and you can reach it standing on dry ground.
  • Never relight a pilot or restore power to a heater that has been sitting in water.
  • Stay back from steam and standing water, because tank water can be hot enough to scald.

Water near wiring or a gas line

If the water is touching outlets, a panel or the heater's own wiring, do not step into it. Call the utility or 911 first for any gas or electrical risk. We can start extraction once the area is safe to enter.

Where leaking tanks do their damage

Many South Gate homes keep the water heater in a garage, a hall closet or a utility alcove. Garage slabs let water run toward the door or into walls, while closet leaks soak baseboards and slip under flooring in the next room. Carpet and drywall wick water upward, so damage reaches higher than the puddle suggests.

Water that pools for a day or more can pick up dirt and debris and shift from clean supply water toward a dirtier category. The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, the published industry reference for professional water damage restoration, uses those categories to decide what is dried in place and what is removed.

Insurance and a failed water heater

A tank that bursts or fails suddenly is the kind of sudden and accidental event homeowners policies often cover, while a leak that dripped for months is often treated as gradual and may not be. Check your policy or ask your insurer before you decide what to claim. We keep moisture readings and dated photos to support your own claim.
