How our water damage services fit together

A water loss moves through stages, and each stage has its own service. First the water is stopped and removed, then the structure is dried, then wet debris is cleaned out, and last the damaged rooms are rebuilt. Mold work is a separate service that applies when moisture has stayed too long.

If you are not sure which stage you are in, call (213) 776-0579 and describe what you see. The dispatcher can tell you where to start. The homepage covers the full water damage restoration process in South Gate.
